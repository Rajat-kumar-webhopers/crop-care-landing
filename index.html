From Murthal to Derabassi

A Sturdy Foundation Built On Farmer Trust & B2B Excellence

Originated with the Registrar of Companies Delhi in 1989. Today powered by dual ultra-modern manufacturing units located in Punjab.

Crop Care Pesticides (India) Pvt. Ltd. was incorporated in the year 1989 with registrar of company Delhi, to Manufacture agro chemicals. Our sole objective of the organization at that time was to cater to our esteemed B2B clientele.

Our original plant was situated in Murthal, Sonipat, which later on moved to Derabassi, Punjab. Now we have added an additional state-of-the-art manufacturing facility in Derabassi, Punjab to address rising demands — both National and Inter-National. We are proud of our strong B2B presence in the agrochemical industry.

timeline Our Heritage

The Best Farming Products Since 1989

In the year 1994, Late Shri Ashok Barpujari led the company to a sturdy and exceptional position to address the ever-growing complexity of the challenges farmers face. The company shifted and expanded to Derabassi, Distt. Mohali, Punjab to pioneer modern crop care.

1989

Incorporation

Incorporated with Registrar of Companies Delhi. Initial manufacturing plant established at Murthal, Sonipat for specialized B2B agrochemicals.

1994

Visionary Guidance

Late Shri Ashok Barpujari led strategic restructuring, steering CCPL toward scalable solutions for agrarian pests and disease complexes.

2004

Unit 1 Derabassi

Manufacturing shifted and expanded to Focal Point, Derabassi, Punjab with modernized liquid formulation and automated bottling capabilities.

2016

Unit 2 Expansion

Commissioned second high-capacity unit in Village Nimbuan, Tehsil Derabassi to serve surging National & International agricultural demands.

2025

Grower Advisory

Direct field consultant network monitoring crop health across Punjab, Haryana, UP, and Rajasthan with regular farmer education programs.
